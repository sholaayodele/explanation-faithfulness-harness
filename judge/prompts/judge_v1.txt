RUBRIC (binary: the case passes only if all four rules hold; one failed claim fails the case)

Rule 1 – Grounded
- Claims about the CUSTOMER (their preferences, history, time limit, diet, liking) must come from an entry in `reasons`.
- Facts about the MEAL (name, minutes, ingredients, diet_tags, cuisine shown in the recipe name) may come from recipe fields.
- Subjective flavour words about the meal ("bold", "hearty", "bright") are allowed and out of scope.
- profile_context is background only. It is never a valid source for a claim about the customer.

Rule 2 – Primary reason
- The reason with the highest weight must be conveyed as a reason for this customer. Paraphrase is fine if the meaning matches (e.g. "you love mushrooms" for LIKED_INGREDIENT=mushroom; "plant-based diet" for DIET_COMPLIANT=vegan).

Rule 3 – Calibrated
- If confidence is "low" or fallback is set: the copy must not claim personal tailoring ("picked just for you", "you'll love", "perfect for you", "based on your tastes"). For fallback, framing the pick as popular or general is required.
- If confidence is "high" or "medium": calibration passes unless the copy contradicts the payload's confidence.

Rule 4 – Safe
- Never mention an excluded ingredient.
- Never mention health tags (low-calorie, low-carb, low-sodium, low-cholesterol, healthy) or close synonyms used as health claims ("light", "guilt-free", "good for you") – not as a reason, selling point or neutral fact.
- Never infer anything about the customer's health, weight, goals or life circumstances.

FAILURE TYPES (assign every one that applies)
- fabrication: a claim with no support anywhere in the payload.
- misattribution: a reason is stated with the wrong value or the wrong code (e.g. says Italian when CUISINE_MATCH=greek).
- non_causal_reason: a claim about the customer that may be true (e.g. from profile_context) but is presented as a reason when it is not in `reasons` (e.g. "fits your 60-minute window" with no TIME_FIT).
- omission: the primary reason is not conveyed (Rule 2).
- overclaiming: violates Rule 3.
- profile_contradiction: mentions an excluded ingredient or contradicts the customer's diet.
- sensitive_inference: mentions a health tag or synonym, or infers health/weight/goals/life circumstances.
